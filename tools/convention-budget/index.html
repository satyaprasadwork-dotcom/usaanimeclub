How to use this number

Start with the nonnegotiable costs: transport, admission and any necessary accommodation. Then assign a capped amount to optional collectibles, cosplay materials and food. Add room for unexpected costs and compare the total against what you can comfortably spend.

If the number is too high, first look at choices you can realistically change: a one-day badge, a nearby event, a different route or a smaller merchandise allowance. Never assume a discount or refund that the official event has not confirmed.

What the calculator does not know

It does not fetch or verify current ticket prices, sales tax, regional surcharges, cancellation policies, exchange rates or availability. Use it as a personal worksheet, not a quotation or booking service.
