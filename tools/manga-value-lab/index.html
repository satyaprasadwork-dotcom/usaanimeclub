How the calculation works

Required purchases = your desired standard volumes divided by the number covered per purchase, rounded up. Total = purchases × quoted price + estimated additional costs. This is meaningful only when both options really cover equivalent content.

Use the manga editions guide to compare ISBNs, included chapters, language and trim size.

Reasons not to choose by price alone

Confirm stock, binding quality, digital versus physical formats, your reading comfort and return terms. Borrowing may be preferable if a library confirms that it carries the edition. The tool is not a retailer, streaming aggregator or ISBN database.

For edition differences, consult International ISBN Agency's ISBN assignment guidance.
