About Us

USA Anime Club is an independent fan-run publication about anime developments that matter to viewers in the United States.

We focus on sourced release announcements, North American streaming rights, movie screenings and production updates. We do not publish unrelated lifestyle material or general anime guides on this site.

Our editorial approach

Every news report links to an original dated announcement and states what remains unconfirmed. We never claim to have conducted interviews or attended events without actually doing so.

USA Anime Club Editorial is our publication byline. Questions: satyaprasadwork@gmail.com. Our editorial policy explains how we verify claims.
