About Us

USA Anime Club is an independent editorial publication covering anime, Japanese manga, Korean manhwa and online fiction for readers in the United States.

We cover adaptations, US streaming releases, official English-language book editions, Korean webtoon industry developments and online-serialized fiction. We do not publish unrelated filler or unverified rumors.

Our editorial approach

Every news report links to an original dated announcement and states what remains unconfirmed. We never claim to have conducted interviews or attended events without actually doing so.

USA Anime Club Editorial is our publication byline. Questions: satyaprasadwork@gmail.com. Our editorial policy explains how we verify claims.
