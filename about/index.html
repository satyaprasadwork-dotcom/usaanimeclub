USA ANIME CLUB

About Us

USA Anime Club is an independent publication for US readers following Japanese anime and manga, Korean manhwa, and online-serialized fiction. We cover announcements that affect what readers can watch, read or buy, with links to the organizations making those announcements.

What we cover

Our reporting includes new anime adaptations, confirmed streaming plans, English manga and manhwa editions, webtoon industry developments and serialized fiction releases. We explain the US relevance of an announcement and distinguish a Japanese broadcast, a regional streaming license, a digital chapter and a collected print volume.

Start with the latest reports or browse Anime, Manga, Manhwa and Web Novels.

How we report

Articles identify their source and its date, summarize the confirmed development, and explain important unanswered questions. Official publisher, distributor, broadcaster and platform records are our starting points. A release date outside the United States is not presented as confirmed US availability unless the source says so.

Our Editorial Policy describes sourcing, attribution and independent editorial responsibility. If a factual statement needs attention, the Corrections Policy explains how to request a review.

Publication and contact

USA Anime Club Editorial is the publication byline on our reports; it is not a claim about a named reporter's credentials or firsthand attendance. USA Anime Club is not affiliated with the anime studios, publishers, streaming platforms or fictional properties it covers.

For story leads, feedback or rights inquiries, email satyaprasadwork@gmail.com. We welcome relevant information, but sending a suggestion does not guarantee coverage.
