Send a news tip

We welcome relevant announcements about anime, manga, manhwa and web novels, especially developments with a confirmed US audience or distribution impact. A useful submission includes:

  • The official announcement or publisher page, not just a repost.
  • The announcement date, the people or organizations involved, and the applicable country or territory.
  • Release formats, platforms or schedules only where a source confirms them.

Submissions are considered editorially. They are not automatically published, endorsed or described as independently reported until checked.

Request a correction

Include the exact article URL, the sentence you believe is inaccurate, the correct information and a dated supporting source. Our Corrections Policy explains how changes are reviewed and noted.

Rights, privacy and accessibility

For a copyright or trademark concern, identify the page and material, explain your relationship to the rights holder, and provide a way to follow up. For accessibility problems, tell us the page and what failed; device or browser details are useful but optional. For personal-data questions, see the Privacy Policy.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, identity documents or personal information about others. We do not advertise a phone line, response deadline or physical newsroom address.
