Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

We do not require accounts or process payment information.

Optional audience measurement

Google Analytics 4 (G-G0TNMR0FQL) and Microsoft Clarity (yyqq9cryjc) only load after a visitor chooses Accept analytics. You can choose Decline analytics instead. Analytics may process site interactions, device data, cookies, approximate network location and session interaction recordings subject to provider masking. We use this for aggregate understanding of the site, not individual identification.

Changing consent

We save your choice in the browser's local storage. Select Privacy choices in any footer to change it. Declining after a previous acceptance reloads the page to stop further tracking; it does not automatically erase prior records held by providers.

Other services

Google Fonts receives basic connection data when font files are requested. Articles link to third-party sites with their own policies. Read Google's privacy policy and Microsoft's privacy statement.

Email and reader requests

When you contact us by email, we use the message to handle the request. We do not sell reader data or run targeted ads on this version of the site. Depending on applicable law you may have access and deletion rights. Write to satyaprasadwork@gmail.com.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13. Please do not send personal information about children.
