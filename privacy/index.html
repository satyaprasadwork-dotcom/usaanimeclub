Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This notice describes how the current USA Anime Club website handles information. You can read articles without creating an account or providing payment details.

Analytics are optional

The site's consent panel offers Accept analytics and Decline analytics. Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-G0TNMR0FQL) and Microsoft Clarity (project ID yyqq9cryjc) are loaded by our site code only after acceptance. They can measure page visits, device and browser characteristics, navigation and interactions. Clarity may create session recordings subject to its masking and privacy controls; analytics providers may use cookies or similar technologies.

Declining keeps these optional analytics scripts from being loaded by the website. The current site code does not load an advertising network. Advertising and its related consent requirements would need separate review before introduction.

Your choice and browser storage

Your decision is stored locally in your browser so the consent panel does not appear on every visit. Select Privacy choices in the footer to change it. If you previously accepted and then decline, the page reloads to stop further analytics loading; this does not erase information already collected by a provider. Clearing your browser's storage may also reset your choice.

The live clock uses the time zone configured on your device. It does not request GPS coordinates or precise location permission.

Other services and links

The site requests font files from Google Fonts, which may receive ordinary connection details such as your IP address. Hosting and network providers may process technical access logs to deliver and protect the website. Articles may link to publishers and external services whose own privacy notices apply when you visit them. See Google's Privacy Policy and Microsoft's Privacy Statement.

Email enquiries and privacy requests

If you email us, your address and message are used to review and respond to the enquiry, and may remain in our email records. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information. For a question about your information or a rights request under applicable law, contact satyaprasadwork@gmail.com. We do not sell readers' personal information.

Children

USA Anime Club is a general-interest publication, not a service directed at children under 13. Please do not send personal information about children through editorial email.
