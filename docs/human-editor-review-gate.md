# Human editorial fact-check and publication sign-off

This is a checklist for an actual human reviewer. Filling it using a model or an automated link check is not a signed human review. The review record in `content/review-register.json` remains **requires_manual_claim_review** until an identifiable human editor performs and documents the checks.

## One guide at a time

1. Read the entire rendered article in a browser. Identify factual, legal, medical, financial, venue-policy, ISBN, pricing, availability, safety and rights claims.
2. Open the exact primary source page, compare its words and applicability, record source URL, relevant section and checked date.
3. If a source is unavailable to automation (HTTP 403 or bot challenge), verify it in an ordinary browser, not by claiming it was inaccessible to readers.
4. Distinguish an original documented comparison or calculation from a hypothetical example. Do not insert photographs, attendance, experience or measurements that were not produced.
5. Inspect every visual against the asset provenance list, including generated anime-inspired material that could resemble existing characters or protected designs.
6. Check factual correction needs, text similarity, headings, links, captions, mobile rendering and whether the guide answers its reader's real problem.
7. Only then fill the reviewer's real name, review date, claim evidence links, correction log and rights check; set `status` to `reviewed` when supported.

**Source research performed with drafting/tool assistance is not a signed human review.**
