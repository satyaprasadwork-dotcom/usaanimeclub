Medaka Kuroiwa Season 2 Heads to Crunchyroll in January 2027

Crunchyroll confirms a January 2027 stream of Medaka Kuroiwa Season 2 in North America, plus a December 6 preview at Anime Frontier in Texas.

By USA Anime Club Editorial · Published November 14, 2026 · Updated November 14, 2026

In brief: The second season is confirmed for Crunchyroll in North America in January 2027. A preview screening is scheduled for December 6 at Anime Frontier in Fort Worth.

What has been confirmed

Crunchyroll announced on November 11 that it will carry the second season of Medaka Kuroiwa Is Impervious to My Charms when the anime premieres in January 2027. The distributor explicitly lists North America among the streaming territories, which makes the announcement relevant to viewers in the United States. The announcement is about streaming rights and a premiere month; it is not yet an episode-by-episode release calendar.

Crunchyroll also confirmed a preview screening at Anime Frontier in Fort Worth, Texas, on December 6. That is a distinct event from the January streaming premiere. Attending a convention screening may require an event badge and could be subject to a separate schedule or capacity policy, so visitors should check the convention's official information before making travel plans.

Why this matters for US viewers

For fans who watched the first season, the most useful detail is distribution certainty. With North American rights announced, there is no need to rely on speculative regional listings to know the intended US streaming home. However, this does not establish the exact local release hour, the full subtitle-language lineup, or whether an English dub will arrive at the same time.

The series is a school-set romantic comedy, and the second season follows the first television run from January through March 2025. Fans following the original manga can also find an official English-language edition through Kodansha USA, as identified in Crunchyroll's announcement. The television anime and printed manga are separate products with different release schedules.

What to watch next

The three checkpoints are an official dated episode schedule, any English-dub announcement, and details of the December Texas screening. If a social post claims a precise January day or US streaming time before a platform announcement, treat that as unconfirmed. A confirmed premiere month is valuable news, but it is not the same as a confirmed minute-by-minute simulcast.
