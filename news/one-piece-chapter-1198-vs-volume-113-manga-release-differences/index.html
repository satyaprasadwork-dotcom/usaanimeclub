One Piece Chapter 1198 vs. Volume 113: A Guide to the Two Release Schedules

One Piece Chapter 1198 appeared November 8, 2026, while VIZ's English Volume 113 released November 10. Learn why serial chapters and books use different calendars.

桜
USA Anime Club Editorial

Editor-in-Chief: Satya Prasad

Published November 14, 2026 · Updated November 15, 2026

QUICK ANSWER

VIZ dates the digital One Piece Chapter 1198 to November 8, 2026, and lists the 208-page English Volume 113 for November 10. A new simulpub chapter and an English collected volume are two separate products at very different points in the story.

Source record: November 8, 2026 · Read the original source

WHAT TO CHECK NEXT

Why do the latest weekly chapter and newest US volume have different numbers?

Keep Japanese serialization, official digital chapters and English collected-book schedules separate when choosing where to resume.

Two November dates, two kinds of publication

VIZ's official One Piece chapter page lists Chapter 1198 for November 8, 2026, while its English Volume 113 product record gives November 10, 2026. These releases are two days apart on a calendar but they are not adjacent chapters of the same product. One is a newly serialized installment available through a digital chapter interface; the other is a collected English book bringing together earlier material. The distinction explains why a person can see two correct dates when searching for the newest One Piece release.

Eiichiro Oda is credited as creator of the work in both publisher listings. The book record identifies the SHONEN JUMP imprint, 208 pages and ISBN 978-1-9747-6670-3. The digital chapter listing identifies installments by chapter number rather than by a retail ISBN. For a reader making a purchase, confirming which of those formats a headline describes is more important than treating the later date as automatically indicating later story events.

What Chapter 1198 means for ongoing readers

The VIZ Shonen Jump directory shows Chapter 1198 as a free recent entry dated November 8, preceded by Chapter 1197 on November 1 and Chapter 1196 on October 18. That sequence is a direct statement about serial publication history. It is useful for a reader keeping up with installments and for distinguishing the official translation from copied or speculative posts circulating before the publisher's release. A chapter-number search is not an invitation to manufacture plot revelations.

VIZ's interface also flags older entries as requiring a membership while sending some free recent chapters through its partner MANGA Plus by SHUEISHA. A user may encounter different access buttons depending on location and platform. The meaningful information is the official page's current offer, not an assumption that everything is permanently free or that a paper volume purchase unlocks an entire online back catalog. Individual chapter access belongs to the reading service's rules.

What the English Volume 113 includes as a product

VIZ describes Volume 113 through a story synopsis involving Luffy, Loki, the World Government's Holy Knights and danger affecting the giants' children on Elbaph. Those are publisher-provided indicators of the book's setting, not a complete scene list. The English paperback is recorded as 208 pages with a November 10 release, while a separate digital listing may carry its own price and eISBN. That metadata allows readers to identify the correct edition even where store thumbnails are outdated.

A collected volume typically groups chapters already serialized earlier in Japan. It is not reasonable to infer that the 208-page book contains Chapter 1198 simply because the standalone chapter appeared two days before the US book date. Publishing pipelines for translation, print preparation, retail logistics and new digital installments operate independently. The precise chapter range should be checked from the edition's contents or publisher records rather than guessed from a convenient arithmetic formula.

Why the story timeline and release timeline diverge

Manga news often uses the word latest without identifying its unit. For a collector, latest may mean the newest English paperback physically stocked in the United States. For someone reading a Japanese-synchronized official digital serial, it may mean the most recently released weekly installment. Those statements can be correct at the same time because the translated book collection normally follows behind the ongoing chapter feed. The release calendar therefore cannot be used as a plot chronology.

This is particularly important for One Piece because individual story arcs span many chapters and books. A person reading the Elbaph portion through an English volume may see digital headlines referring to later-numbered chapters. Clicking a spoiler-heavy summary because it shares a November date could reveal events not present in the book collection. A publication guide can help protect the reading order by naming the format and chapter or volume number before discussing storyline.

Choosing between weekly reading and a collected edition

Readers who want to follow discussion shortly after a new chapter appears should start at the licensed Shonen Jump or MANGA Plus listing. VIZ's chapter directory can show which installments are free and which need membership. Someone who wants a durable shelf collection can choose an English paperback or digital edition with the relevant ISBN. Both choices support authorized releases but answer different needs. A streaming video subscription does not substitute for either manga-reading product.

VIZ's Shonen Jump membership is described separately from the purchase of volumes, and MANGA Plus supplies its own app-only First Read Free option for eligible series. Restrictions on rereading, platform, language and territory should be checked before relying on any offer. Choosing a service based solely on the label free may be misleading if the reader intends to catch up on hundreds of chapters or return to a particular page repeatedly.

How to verify a release claim before sharing it

A trustworthy One Piece update needs a clear noun after the word release. If the claim concerns Chapter 1198, look for the VIZ chapter list and its November 8 record. If it concerns Volume 113, consult the distinct product page, November 10 date, 208-page specification and ISBN. A screenshot without its page address may refer to a Japanese edition, a free chapter, an imported book or an old preorder. It should not become evidence of a new American release on its own.

Readers comparing bookstore listings should also expect different pricing for paperback and digital formats. Retail stock, local taxes and delivery fees are merchant conditions, not a change in the manga's official publication sequence. If a store has a delayed shipment of Volume 113, that delay does not move the digital release of Chapter 1198. Stating these events independently prevents a routine logistics question from being misreported as a postponement of the series.

What to track next

The next informative digital update would be a new dated chapter entry or a revised publication break recorded by the official service. The next collected-edition update would identify a later English volume and its own publisher metadata. A new box set is yet another product, bundling earlier books rather than creating newly serialized material. Combining all these into one unspecified One Piece calendar makes the guide less accurate for people with specific needs.

As of the November review, the answer is straightforward: Chapter 1198 was listed on November 8 in VIZ's official serial directory; English Volume 113 released November 10 as a 208-page SHONEN JUMP book. The physical or digital collection and the fresh serial chapter should be checked using their respective official records. That format distinction is the central fact, and it remains useful long after the surrounding search trends move on.
