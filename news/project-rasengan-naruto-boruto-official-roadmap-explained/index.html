Project Rasengan Explained: The Confirmed Future of Naruto and Boruto

Project Rasengan is Naruto's official multiyear roadmap. Learn the confirmed Shadow Rising manga, Ninkū anime, worldwide mobile game and June 2027 card launch.

桜
USA Anime Club Editorial

Editor-in-Chief: Satya Prasad

Published November 15, 2026 · Updated November 15, 2026 at 3:36 PM UTC

QUICK ANSWER

Project Rasengan is an official multiyear, cross-media Naruto franchise initiative unveiled October 10, 2026. Publicly named components include Naruto × Ninkū, the Shadow Rising manga, the global Naruto Mobile expansion and Naruto Card Game in June 2027. Additional roadmap entries remain undisclosed; the graphic is not a timetable for Boruto Anime Part 2.

Source record: October 10, 2026 · Read the original source

WHAT TO CHECK NEXT

What exactly is confirmed under Project Rasengan?

Officially named projects include Naruto × Ninkū, Kishimoto's Shadow Rising manga, a worldwide Naruto Mobile expansion and Bandai's June 2027 Naruto Card Game. Other roadmap entries remain unannounced.

A franchise roadmap, not the title of one anime

Project Rasengan was introduced during New York Comic Con on October 10, 2026 as a long-term international initiative for Naruto and Boruto. VIZ's US press release describes it as a plan across manga, animation, games and additional activities, accompanied by a promotional film and a graphic roadmap. The official landing page groups announced projects into separate modules. That makes Project Rasengan an umbrella for several works, not the title of a single television season.

This matters for search results. Someone encountering the phrase Project Rasengan anime release date may actually be asking about Naruto × Ninkū, while a card collector is interested in a June 2027 launch. A general roadmap article should guide readers to those distinct facts and avoid assigning one date to the entire project. Each component has its own producer, medium and announcement status.

Confirmed component: Naruto × Ninkū

The announced anime component is Naruto × Ninkū, a four-episode original crossover supervised by Naruto creator Masashi Kishimoto and Ninkū creator Koji Kiriyama. Its confirmed premise sends young Team 7 on a mission into another world where they encounter Fūsuke. The project has a dedicated official website with the story introduction and creator comments, so it is more substantive than a single mystery panel on a roadmap slide.

Nevertheless, Project Rasengan's existence does not provide a Japanese broadcast premiere, a Netflix date or a confirmed US streaming service for the crossover. A full story synopsis beyond the official introduction and claims about future guest characters would be speculation. Fans should follow the crossover-specific page for episode availability, because a multiyear marketing campaign can continue even while a particular anime has no public broadcast schedule.

Confirmed component: the Shadow Rising manga

New Naruto: Shadow Rising manga by Masashi Kishimoto is another revealed component. The Japanese Naruto announcement identifies the title and describes teaser images involving the Hokage monument and members of Team 7. The official Project Rasengan page presents it as a new story drawn by Kishimoto. That is a distinct creative promise from his supervision role on Boruto: Two Blue Vortex, which uses Mikio Ikemoto's ongoing art.

Shadow Rising has not been assigned an official serial publication day, issue number, collected-volume date or licensed US Chapter 1 URL in the records checked. The teaser's dramatic images are not an invitation to create fictional chapter summaries. More information is promised at Jump Festa 2027 in December. The manga article can explain the teaser in detail, while this roadmap guide focuses on how it fits into the wider set of announced projects.

Confirmed component: a worldwide Naruto Card Game

Bandai has confirmed that the Naruto Card Game will launch internationally in June 2027. The planned opening product family includes three preconstructed starter decks built around Naruto, Sasuke and Gaara, as well as an initial booster set. Official materials describe tactical card play using leaders, shinobi and chakra. Unlike a speculative roadmap box, this project has its own product website and a concrete worldwide launch month.

That does not establish every local store's inventory, retailer price or exact shipment day. June 2027 is the official release window, not permission to invent a Saturday on the calendar or a presale quantity. Demo activities have been announced for earlier in 2027, but participation can depend on stores and event eligibility. A card guide should provide those practical distinctions, leaving the broader Project Rasengan page focused on the franchise-wide chronology.

Confirmed component: Naruto Mobile expansion

The existing Naruto Mobile action game has operated in mainland China since 2016. The October 2026 showcase announced plans to take it worldwide, accompanied by promotional material and promised future updates. The project is listed on the official Rasengan page as a game that will expand internationally rather than an entirely unannounced new television series. Its release plan is commercially meaningful because American players have long encountered a Chinese-language market version.

The announcement includes important limitations. An exact global rollout date was not provided, supported languages have not been finalized publicly, and the franchise's note says availability may vary by country or region. That means a worldwide initiative is not identical to a guarantee that a particular US app store will contain the game on a stated day. Read the individual game guide for practical checks before installing any purported early APK or unofficial client.

The blank roadmap slots are intentionally unresolved

Promotional roadmaps can imply a great deal through empty panels, silhouettes and future years. Project Rasengan's 2026 announcement indicated that additional projects were still unannounced. It is reasonable to say that the franchise has more plans than those already named. It is not reasonable to label a blank space Boruto Season 2, a Hollywood sequel or a specific game expansion without an official identification.

Nor should a general 2027–2029 planning graphic be treated as an episode calendar. A block positioned near a year may represent a broad investment horizon rather than a premiere or publication day. Until the rights holder assigns text and a date to that position, a reader has only an open question. That distinction prevents the error of turning an attractive promotional graphic into apparently precise but invented journalism.

Where Boruto anime Part 2 fits—and does not fit

The Boruto anime committee announced Part 2 as in production in March 2023 after identifying Episode 293 as the first part's finale. That is a separate primary-source fact. The October 2026 Project Rasengan announcements name a new Naruto crossover, manga and games, but do not set a confirmed Boruto Part 2 premiere date in the materials examined. A roadmap carrying both Naruto and Boruto branding is not sufficient to resolve the television continuation's schedule.

Anyone searching for the Boruto return date should consult the standalone anime status report, which cites the actual committee statement. Manga publication under the Two Blue Vortex title continues through VIZ's English chapter service and has different release rules. Combining an ongoing manga with an old production announcement and an unlabeled promotional slot would create a false schedule even if the three facts are each individually genuine.

Jump Festa 2027 as the next editorial checkpoint

The official Naruto announcements point readers to Jump Festa 2027 for follow-up reveals. The event runs December 19–20, 2026; the organizer places Naruto in the Saturday program, 16:55–17:25 JST, on December 19. That is a reliable time for checking actual public announcements, not a guaranteed launch of every project shown on the October roadmap.

A useful publication strategy is to maintain a source-linked register of which projects have named titles, rights holders, dates and US availability. When the December stage changes one of those fields, update the corresponding focused article and this overview with a new verified modified timestamp. Readers then receive a coherent family of guides instead of eleven announcements that all imply the same release date or invent details to fill every open roadmap box.
