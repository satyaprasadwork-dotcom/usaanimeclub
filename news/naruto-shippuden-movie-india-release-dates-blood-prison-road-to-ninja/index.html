Naruto Shippuden India Release Dates: What’s Confirmed for Blood Prison and Road to Ninja

Blood Prison and Road to Ninja are planned for Indian cinemas, but no exact date is confirmed. Latest on Hindi audio and an unverified CBFC claim.

桜
USA Anime Club Editorial

Editor-in-Chief: Satya Prasad

Published November 21, 2026 · Updated November 21, 2026

QUICK ANSWER

IGN India reported on September 3 that Naruto Shippuden films Blood Prison and Road to Ninja are planned for Indian cinemas, with Hindi and Japanese-language options. No exact India release date or ticket booking date has been verified. A later claim that Road to Ninja received a CBFC certificate and is coming from PVR INOX could not be matched to an official certificate, so it remains unconfirmed.

Source record: September 3, 2026 · Read the original source

WHAT TO CHECK NEXT

What would count as a confirmed India release date?

Look for a dated announcement from the Indian distributor or cinema chain, then confirm that the same date appears on a ticketing page for your city. A CBFC certificate by itself does not give a show date.

The short answer: no exact India date yet

The news is real, but the date fans are searching for has not been confirmed. IGN India reported on September 3, 2026 that Naruto Shippuden: Blood Prison and Road to Ninja were planned for Indian cinemas. The report described Hindi-dubbed and Japanese-language viewing options, but it did not name a release day, month, booking start, distributor, or city-by-city schedule.

As of November 21, 2026, I could not verify an official India release date or a live ticket listing for either film. That means there is no reliable countdown to share yet. If a social post gives a specific Friday or says bookings are open, check whether an Indian distributor, cinema chain, or ticketing page carries the same announcement before treating it as official.

Naruto Shippuden India release dates: the confirmed status

The September report points to a theatrical plan for both titles, not a fully published release schedule. The table below separates what the report says from what is still missing. “Not announced” refers to the India release date; it does not change the films’ old Japanese premiere dates, which are listed separately further down.

The available reporting does not establish whether the films will open on the same day, arrive in different weeks, or receive limited event screenings before a wider run. It also does not confirm which cities will be included. Until a dated listing appears from the people handling the Indian release, both India dates should be treated as unannounced.

What was announced at Anime India Mumbai?

IGN India tied the announcement to Anime India Mumbai, where Naruto anime director Hayato Date appeared. Filmfare also reported on Date’s participation, and the event’s official agenda listed his sessions during the Mumbai convention. The agenda is useful confirmation that Date was scheduled at the event; it does not itself announce the movie screenings or their dates.

The report says the planned cinema versions include a Hindi dub as well as Japanese audio with English subtitles. That is the most useful language information currently attached to the news. It is still a report of the planned options rather than a final booking page for a particular cinema, so fans should wait for a distributor or exhibitor to confirm the exact versions and showtimes.

A newer Road to Ninja certificate claim remains unverified

A later AnimeHub India page says Road to Ninja received a UA 16+ theatrical certificate on October 15, 2026, lists a runtime of 111.3 minutes, and names PVR INOX Pictures as the applicant and distributor. The same page describes the Indian version as Hindi-dubbed. Those are specific claims, but the page’s button labelled as a certificate link led to its own announcements page rather than a CBFC certificate that could be checked.

I could not independently match the page’s stated certificate number, DFL/7/31/2026-MUM, to a public CBFC record. For that reason, the rating, runtime, Hindi-only version, and PVR INOX role should be treated as unverified—not as a confirmed release announcement. Even if a film has received certification, certification alone does not tell audiences when tickets go on sale or when a theatre will screen it.

The claim also does not provide an India release date. Until an official CBFC record, PVR INOX announcement, or ticketing listing confirms the details, do not use October 15 as a release date: the page presents it as a certificate date, not a cinema date.

The exact Japanese release dates are already known

Both films have been available for years in Japan, and their original premiere dates are easy to confuse with the new India news. Studio Pierrot’s official Blood Prison page gives July 30, 2011 as its Japanese release date. TV Tokyo’s official Road to Ninja page identifies July 28, 2012 as that film’s theatrical opening in Japan.

These dates are historical Japan premieres, not India dates, re-releases, or promised screening days for 2026. A search result that combines the movie titles with “release date” may show 2011 or 2012 without specifying the country. Check the location in the source before assuming the result answers when an Indian cinema will show the film.

What are Blood Prison and Road to Ninja about?

Blood Prison sends Naruto to Hozuki Castle after he is accused of crimes he did not commit. The isolated prison strips him of much of his ability to use chakra, and he must work out why he was framed while surviving inside a place designed to prevent escape. Its prison setting gives the film a more contained mystery-and-escape structure than a typical village mission.

Road to Ninja puts Naruto and Sakura in a distorted version of the world they know, where the people around them and their family circumstances are different. It uses that alternate-world premise to explore what Naruto and Sakura value about their friends and home. They are separate movie stories, so Indian exhibitors can schedule them as individual screenings rather than as a two-part narrative.

Hindi dub, subtitles, tickets, and cities: what is still open

The reported Hindi dub is likely to be the detail many Indian fans care about most, but the available announcement does not provide a finished language-by-language booking chart. IGN India’s report says Hindi and Japanese audio with English subtitles are planned. It does not establish Tamil, Telugu, or other dubbed options for these two theatrical versions, even though Naruto has received regional-language releases in other formats.

No theatre chain has been independently confirmed here as the release partner, and I could not verify city listings, showtimes, prices, age-rating details, or a ticket-sale date. The PVR INOX claim belongs to the unverified AnimeHub page above. Treat a ticket portal as confirmation only when the title, city, venue, date, and version are visible on the cinema or booking service itself.

Where to check for the next update

For a useful update, look for a post with a specific date from the Indian distributor or an official cinema chain account, then check the corresponding ticket page. A poster without an exhibitor, city, or booking link can show that promotion has begun, but it does not prove that tickets are on sale. A CBFC record can confirm certification details, but it is not a substitute for a cinema schedule.

The first update that would change this article is a verifiable date for Blood Prison or Road to Ninja in India. The next useful details would be whether both films share a release day, which cities receive screenings, the confirmed audio and subtitle options, and when advance booking opens. Until those details appear in a traceable source, the accurate answer remains: Indian theatrical releases are planned, exact dates are not confirmed.
