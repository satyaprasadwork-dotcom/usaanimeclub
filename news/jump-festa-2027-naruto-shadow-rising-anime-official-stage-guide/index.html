Jump Festa 2027: Naruto Shadow Rising, New Anime and the Announcements to Watch

Jump Festa 2027 runs December 19–20, 2026. Naruto's official stage is December 19, 16:55–17:25 JST; track confirmed follow-ups for Shadow Rising and Project Rasengan.

桜
USA Anime Club Editorial

Editor-in-Chief: Satya Prasad

Published November 15, 2026 · Updated November 15, 2026 at 3:38 PM UTC

QUICK ANSWER

Jump Festa 2027 takes place December 19–20, 2026 at Makuhari Messe in Japan. The official Naruto Super Stage is scheduled on Saturday December 19 from 16:55 to 17:25 JST, with Junko Takeuchi, Noriaki Sugiyama and Chie Nakamura listed. Naruto's October 2026 rights-holder update promises follow-up information on the new Shadow Rising manga and other Project Rasengan developments, but no exact anime premieres or manga publication date can be assumed beforehand.

Source record: October 11, 2026 · Read the original source

WHAT TO CHECK NEXT

When is the Naruto stage at Jump Festa 2027 and what should fans expect?

The official Naruto stage is Saturday December 19, 2026 from 16:55 to 17:25 JST. Follow-up Shadow Rising and Project Rasengan information is expected, but exact new release dates are not predetermined.

Jump Festa 2027 actually happens in December 2026

The name Jump Festa 2027 can mislead readers checking a calendar. The official event is scheduled for Saturday December 19 and Sunday December 20, 2026 at Makuhari Messe in Japan. Those are the actual calendar dates for the event branded with the following year. Naruto is among the organizer's first-day Super Stage lineup. The venue and stage information come directly from the organizer, not an event listing copied from an older edition.

For US readers this is especially important because time-zone differences can make Japanese announcements arrive while it is still December 18 in parts of North America. The safest editorial approach is to show the official Japan Standard Time slot first and provide any American conversion clearly labeled by location. A social post that treats the event branding as a December 2027 date would send fans to the wrong year.

The Naruto stage has an exact official slot

The organizer's program assigns Naruto to the December 19 afternoon slot, running 16:55–17:25 JST. The lineup names Junko Takeuchi, the Japanese voice of Naruto, Noriaki Sugiyama, the voice of Sasuke, and Chie Nakamura, the voice of Sakura. Their appearance connects the presentation to the original Team 7 identity at a time when new projects focus on that earlier group.

Those names and the thirty-minute schedule are confirmed; the complete sequence of announcements within the stage is not. A stage can feature conversation, promotional videos, future projects and nostalgic performances without allocating equal time to every item fans hope to hear about. Editors should not publish a detailed announcement timetable for unannounced products simply because their teaser images appeared at New York Comic Con.

The new Shadow Rising manga is the clearest promised follow-up

The Naruto franchise's October 11 notice says details about Masashi Kishimoto's newly announced Shadow Rising manga should follow at Jump Festa's Naruto stage. That makes it the strongest editorial checkpoint for anyone following the manga's publishing status. The October teaser featured Hokage monument imagery, Team 7 and a Naruto-Sasuke confrontation, but left its publication timing and format unstated. An actual Jump Festa notice could fill some of those gaps.

However, the promise of further details is not itself a guarantee of a Chapter 1 release on December 19, a serial magazine announcement or simultaneous English publication. A credible preview should distinguish the questions that remain open from confirmed event activity. If the panel reveals only an additional visual, that would still be new information, but not proof that the book is immediately available for purchase.

Naruto × Ninkū may receive an update, but the premiere remains unknown

The four-episode Naruto × Ninkū crossover has an established title, story premise and creative supervision by Kishimoto and Koji Kiriyama. Because it was part of the October Project Rasengan presentation, readers will naturally look for further information at the next major franchise event. Yet the material reviewed for this guide does not promise a specific crossover release date announcement at the December stage.

A responsible preview can list the confirmed crossover facts and identify its Japanese broadcast timing, episode-by-episode schedule and US streaming deal as unanswered questions. It cannot confidently predict a premiere month merely from the number of episodes or from the names of the voice actors appearing at Jump Festa. A date must come from the production committee or distributor. If it is revealed, the dedicated Naruto × Ninkū guide should be revised with the precise source.

Project Rasengan gives the event broader significance

New York Comic Con introduced Project Rasengan as a multiyear global Naruto roadmap covering manga, anime, games and more. The official site displays some concrete products and leaves other parts open. VIZ's US announcement said further updates would come at Jump Festa. This gives the December stage a real purpose beyond franchise nostalgia: it is the next public opportunity to clarify which projects advance and which remain longer-term plans.

At the same time, a graphic with unrevealed boxes should not be treated as an advance announcement of Boruto Part 2, a new film release date or a surprise sequel. Those are possibilities fans may discuss, not source-supported stage commitments. USA Anime Club can maintain a compact confirmation register identifying which named initiatives receive dates or distribution details during the panel, preserving the distinction between a teaser and a formal launch schedule.

Mobile game and trading-card questions to monitor

The Naruto Card Game already has a global June 2027 launch month, three preconstructed starter decks and an initial booster. Its official announcement identifies Jump Festa as a place where more card details may be revealed. Practical follow-ups could include product specifications, event registration or regional launch logistics. None of those should be invented ahead of Bandai's statements. The manufacturer, not a speculative fan price list, is the primary source for product facts.

Naruto Mobile's international expansion was also announced in October. The original notice promises more details at Jump Festa but includes a warning that territory availability and language support can vary. For American players, those distribution details would matter more than a generic trailer. A stage announcement of a broad global plan would still not establish a US app-store launch until the publisher actually supplies a supported region and release date.

How American fans can follow the event responsibly

The organizer maintains an official Jump Festa website with stage information, admission rules and updates about available viewing arrangements. Readers should consult that page before assuming every program will be freely streamed worldwide or that a particular archived video will remain available later. In-person stage admission and remote viewing can have different conditions. Reposts of partial Japanese-language clips may omit dates or qualifications important for understanding the announcement.

A useful after-event workflow is to cross-check the stage's statements against the Naruto official English news page, VIZ's US press materials and the respective product websites. Translate the actual announced dates carefully, and distinguish a Japanese event start time from the US publication date of an accompanying article. Doing so produces a stronger account than publishing several unverified rumor headlines as soon as a visual appears on social media.

What qualifies as an important announcement after the stage

For Shadow Rising, the high-value news would be an exact first-chapter publication date, format, magazine and official English reading arrangements. For Naruto × Ninkū, it would be a broadcast day, channel and US streaming rights. For the Naruto Card Game, a product catalog and regional retail information would be useful. For Naruto Mobile, supported countries and devices would turn a broad promise into an actionable US guide. Every future piece should be anchored to a dated primary statement.

The event itself is already confirmed: December 19–20, 2026, with Naruto's Saturday 16:55–17:25 JST presentation. This article should be updated after the actual stage rather than pretending in November to know its surprises. Its editorial value is to give US fans a trustworthy watchlist of questions while keeping the original manga, anime, games and live-action movie announcements separate.
