Black Clover Season 2: US Streaming Schedule, Subtitles and Dub Status

Crunchyroll lists Black Clover Season 2 for North America, with Saturday subtitled and dubbed episode times. Here is what its current fall schedule actually confirms.

桜
USA Anime Club Editorial

Editor-in-Chief: Satya Prasad

Published November 14, 2026 · Updated November 15, 2026

QUICK ANSWER

Crunchyroll's updated Fall 2026 lineup lists Black Clover Season 2 from Studio Pierrot on Saturdays at 8:00 a.m. Pacific for both the subtitled and dubbed streams, with North America included in the licensed territories. Check the episode listing for the languages currently attached to each installment.

Source record: October 28, 2026 · Read the original source

WHAT TO CHECK NEXT

Does the currently published US schedule cover both audio versions?

Check the most recent official season listing for separate subtitled and dubbed release times; the schedules may change.

The confirmed US schedule

Crunchyroll's Fall 2026 schedule gives Black Clover Season 2 a specific weekly slot: Saturday at 8:00 a.m. Pacific Time. The same updated lineup lists an English-dub airtime of Saturday at 8:00 a.m. Pacific. North America is explicitly included among the territories where the platform offers the series. These details establish a usable US streaming plan rather than a vague statement that new episodes are coming sometime this season.

The schedule also identifies Studio Pierrot as the animation studio. Its synopsis describes Asta and the Magic Knights entering the Spade Kingdom to rescue their captains from the Dark Triad. That description places the release in a particular phase of the story without pretending to summarize episodes a reader may not have watched. A promotional synopsis can show the setting of a season, but it cannot confirm every event, a finale outcome or its precise episode count.

The listed broadcast hour is a Pacific-time reference. A viewer in New York, Texas or another US location should use a current conversion for the date in question, especially near daylight-saving changes. It is better to rely on the platform's episode page than an old screenshot of a countdown. The official listing is the baseline; actual playback availability remains the final practical check.

Subtitled episodes and the English dub

Two lines on a platform schedule can describe different products. A subtitled simulcast generally provides Japanese-language performances with selectable on-screen translation. An English dub contains another recorded performance. Their production schedules, audio tracks and catalog presentation can differ, even when a distributor lists the same release hour. Crunchyroll's updated fall guide identifies both tracks for Black Clover Season 2, but users should still inspect the language choices attached to the episode they intend to watch.

For a US household sharing one account, the difference matters. Someone may want Japanese audio while another viewer expects English. A series-level page can advertise multiple languages while a particular episode is temporarily missing a track, or older seasons may be grouped separately. The safest path is to open the actual new-season episode and check its audio and subtitles menu before planning a watch party around a promised language.

Earlier versions of the fall lineup initially labeled the dub time as unannounced. The updated official page later displayed an explicit Saturday hour. This illustrates why a dated schedule is more reliable than a social-media post repeating an older announcement. Reporting that the dub is still entirely unconfirmed would overlook the later update; promising that all possible languages will arrive simultaneously would go further than the posted information.

Where to find the new season without confusing editions

Black Clover has a substantial existing television catalog, which makes search results less straightforward than a newly launched show with a single season. A viewer may encounter old episode lists, recap videos, compilation clips and the current official continuation in the same search. Use the distributor's Black Clover Season 2 entry or the Watch Now link from its fall lineup, then confirm the season heading and visible episode dates rather than choosing an arbitrary result with the franchise title.

The term Season 2 in a current marketing announcement is a release label, not necessarily evidence that every prior television episode was originally marketed under the same season numbering scheme. Different services and databases sometimes divide long-running anime into arcs or seasons differently. Comparing an episode number from a fan guide directly against a streaming platform's season selector can therefore create a false missing-episode problem.

An official trailer is helpful for recognizing characters and identifying a promotion as current, but it is not an episode page. Likewise, a clip reposted on a video platform does not establish licensed streaming rights in the United States. For access, subscription requirements and playable languages, consult the official service while signed in with the account and region that will actually be used.

What the story announcement does and does not reveal

The Spade Kingdom setting creates an obvious question for returning viewers: how much earlier material should they revisit? Crunchyroll's description confirms the immediate objective of rescuing captains from the Dark Triad. It does not supply an official watch-order shortcut, specify the entire amount of manga covered or identify how many episodes will resolve the conflict. Those larger questions require separate source material or an actual viewing record.

A reader following the manga can identify thematic overlaps with the adaptation, but chapter-to-episode predictions should not be presented as facts from a streaming schedule. Adaptation pacing, editing, extended scenes and broadcast changes can alter where a season ends. An article that labels an unannounced chapter endpoint as confirmed is likely to mislead exactly the reader searching for practical information.

There is also a difference between a series returning and a new US licensing announcement. The fall listing names North America, which directly answers the basic availability question for that service. It does not establish exclusivity, a future home-video release, physical collector editions or whether every older season is available with identical subtitle options. Those would require the relevant distributor or publisher to say so.

A sensible weekly viewing routine

For a regular Saturday viewer, the simplest routine begins with the platform rather than a calendar app. Open Black Clover on Crunchyroll, identify the current Season 2 episode, confirm the displayed publication time and choose an available audio track. If the newest installment has not appeared at the advertised time, recheck the title page and service announcements before treating an unrelated account issue as a cancellation.

The official North American territory listing is important, but a platform listing is not a promise that every travel location will display the same catalog. Someone watching from outside the United States may see different rights. An account registered in the US is not a substitute for checking where the device is actually being used. Regional differences are especially relevant to viewers who share streaming recommendations with friends in other countries.

Episode releases can occasionally shift for production, scheduling or technical reasons. For that reason, a durable guide needs a date-stamped source and a clear distinction between the currently published weekly slot and an unconditional guarantee about future Saturdays. Keeping the wording modest does not make it less useful; it lets readers take action without relying on a prediction disguised as a schedule.

What to check as the season continues

The first follow-up is whether the Saturday subtitle and dub listings remain synchronized. Crunchyroll can update its seasonal guide, while individual episode pages show the tracks actually available. The second is whether production staff or the studio publish additional official information about an episode, pause or special program. Changes should be recorded with their own announcement dates, not silently inserted into an earlier report.

Third, watch for legitimate US distribution news outside the initial simulcast. A confirmed disc edition, language expansion or licensing adjustment may affect readers who do not subscribe to the platform during the season. None is established merely by the presence of the series in a current simulcast table. It is useful to distinguish what can be watched today from what a collector might be able to own later.

As checked against the updated Fall 2026 lineup, the actionable facts are straightforward: Studio Pierrot's Black Clover Season 2 is listed for North America on Crunchyroll, with both subtitle and English-dub slots shown for Saturdays at 8:00 a.m. Pacific. The right next step for a US viewer is to open the current episode listing, not to infer additional schedule details from speculation.
