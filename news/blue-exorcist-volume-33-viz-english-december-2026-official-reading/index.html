Blue Exorcist Volume 33: English Release Date and Official Reading Options

VIZ confirms Blue Exorcist Volume 33 in English for December 1, 2026: paperback ISBN 9781974769087, 202 pages, digital edition and legal chapter access.

桜
USA Anime Club Editorial

Editor-in-Chief: Satya Prasad

Published November 15, 2026 · Updated November 15, 2026 at 1:43 PM UTC

QUICK ANSWER

VIZ lists Blue Exorcist Volume 33 for December 1, 2026 in English, with a 202-page paperback, ISBN 978-1-9747-6908-7, and a separate digital edition. The plot description focuses on Shiemi, the Grigori and Rin; individual serialized chapters have separate access terms through VIZ and MANGA Plus.

Source record: November 15, 2026 · Read the original source

WHAT TO CHECK NEXT

When does Blue Exorcist Volume 33 release in English and how can you read chapters legally?

VIZ lists the English paperback for December 1, 2026 (202 pages, ISBN 9781974769087). Check VIZ's official chapter index and MANGA Plus for separate digital chapter access.

The confirmed December 1 English release

VIZ Media's product listing gives December 1, 2026 as the English publication date for Blue Exorcist Volume 33. The printed book is presented as a paperback under the Shonen Jump imprint, credited entirely to creator Kazue Kato. It has 202 pages and an ISBN-13 of 978-1-9747-6908-7. As of the November 15 check, VIZ displays pre-order links, not evidence that the new volume is already on shelves across the United States. Readers can use that identifier to avoid confusing the licensed English book with a Japanese-language import.

The listing's displayed paperback price is $11.99 with an explicit warning that the amount may vary at individual retailers. A seller's preorder receipt may also show shipping costs or local taxes. That makes the official date and ISBN better reference points than an unqualified claim that every buyer will pay the same total price or receive the book on publication day. A dated buyer's guide should distinguish the publisher's release schedule from the delivery estimate supplied by a particular store.

What the Volume 33 synopsis confirms

VIZ's official description emphasizes Shiemi's lifelong connection to the Grigori and the return of memories associated with training to become Shemihaza. It also describes the deep personal history between Shiemi and Rin, suggesting an emotional conversation amid a wider battle. These elements identify the book's narrative focus without establishing a final romantic outcome or revealing the result of the broader confrontation. The publisher's short summary is a reliable way to orient a returning reader while respecting the fact that the volume was not yet released in English at the time of this report.

A late-numbered Blue Exorcist volume draws on many earlier character relationships. Someone who knows only the initial anime premise should not assume the thirty-third collected book starts a new standalone story. The synopsis refers to institutions, characters and unresolved questions that have been developed over numerous installments. For a spoiler-conscious newcomer, it is better to start at the beginning or revisit the most recently completed volume, rather than searching the internet for an explanation that reveals later developments in full.

Print identifiers and the separate digital option

The VIZ product page explicitly offers paperback and digital formats. The paperback's thirteen-digit ISBN is the edition identifier confirmed above; the digital purchase pathway should be checked on its own format page before assuming it shares the same ISBN or has identical retail conditions. Digital readers may care about compatible apps, account requirements and whether the purchase provides a file or access inside a specific storefront. Those rules are determined by the seller, not by the word digital alone.

Physically collected editions have other advantages: stable volume numbering and a recognizable place on a shelf alongside preceding books. For someone trying to continue a collection, the matching spine number and VIZ Shonen Jump imprint are practical checks. A retailer might present bundles, special imports or unrelated Blue Exorcist media beside the latest book. Confirm author, publisher, volume number and edition before ordering. A listing that says Vol. 33 without the correct language may not satisfy the reader's goal.

Where to read official English chapters

VIZ operates an official Shonen Jump chapter directory for Blue Exorcist and links readers to MANGA Plus by SHUEISHA for current free chapter access where offered. Its English directory displays individual chapter numbers and labels some entries free while others require a membership or purchasing a collected volume. These flags are title and chapter specific. The existence of a free latest chapter does not establish that every older chapter in the series is open without payment, nor that each feature is supported in every country.

MANGA Plus provides another authorized English chapter interface, with its own rules for first-time reading and access to ongoing works. A fan should choose the official series page, verify the specific chapter number, and read the current entitlement displayed for that chapter. The chapter page and the upcoming book have different purposes: one provides serial installments, while the December edition collects a publisher-defined section into a saleable volume. Their release times need not match.

Why a new English volume is not the newest serialized chapter

Blue Exorcist's serialized Japanese and English chapter publication can progress on a different calendar from the collected paperback editions. VIZ's chapter directory shows recent individually numbered chapters that are not the same thing as the number thirty-three printed on a book cover. A frequent source of confusion is treating Volume 33 as if it means Chapter 33, or assuming that a bookstore release on December 1 is the day all manga chapters for that story first appear online.

To decide what to read next, note the last chapter or collected book you finished. If you own the English physical editions, compare your latest spine number with VIZ's complete product catalog. If you read in a digital chapter service, use that service's actual chapter index to resume. An exact conversion between a volume boundary and an anime episode should not be asserted without examining the licensed contents and the episode's final adapted scene.

How the book relates to Blue Exorcist anime

Blue Exorcist has an animated adaptation, but its television arcs and the manga's collected editions are not interchangeable numbering systems. Viewers who have only seen the anime may have encountered the characters through different pacing, episode groupings or publication periods. The Volume 33 synopsis is unmistakably situated deep in the manga continuity, so it should not be marketed as a recommended first chapter for someone who wants only to continue after watching one particular television season.

A precise anime-to-manga handoff requires two pieces of evidence: which animated episode has actually aired and what corresponding story material the authorized manga edition contains. The publisher's current book listing confirms the volume's US sale information, not an episode-by-episode correspondence. Readers can use our separate continuation-method guide to compare works carefully while avoiding fabricated starting chapters. This is more useful than assigning a number based only on similarly named story arcs.

Age guidance, format and collecting checks

VIZ labels the Blue Exorcist Volume 33 book Teen Plus and lists a standard five-by-seven-and-a-half-inch trim size for the paperback. The stated length is 202 pages. These details can matter when someone is buying for a younger reader, matching a uniform collection or comparing a digital format to print. They come directly from the publisher, rather than assumptions about manga as a genre. A product's age guidance can also differ from the classification of a separate animated adaptation.

Before checkout, check the book's language, its numerical position after Volume 32, and the retailer's displayed availability for your US address. If a store offers a preorder, that indicates the seller is accepting orders rather than proving that finished books have already been shipped. If the publisher subsequently changes the release information, refer back to the dated VIZ listing and make a substantive correction rather than silently updating a headline to a different day.

The most useful updates to watch for

After publication, meaningful news would include an official VIZ date correction, a revised product format, a changed ISBN or a material alteration to the retailer distribution information. Another serialized chapter appearing online is relevant to fans but does not automatically change the release record for the December paperback. A report can note where official chapter reading is available without republishing copyrighted manga pages or inventing an early look at an unreleased book.

For a US reader, the concise, source-backed answer is that Blue Exorcist Volume 33 is listed for December 1, 2026, in English, with a 202-page VIZ paperback and ISBN 978-1-9747-6908-7. VIZ also offers a digital edition, while serialized chapters remain available through the appropriate official reading services under separate access terms. The publisher's story description points to Shiemi, Rin and the Grigori, not an entirely new independent story. Those distinctions are the reliable core of a purchasing or reading decision.
