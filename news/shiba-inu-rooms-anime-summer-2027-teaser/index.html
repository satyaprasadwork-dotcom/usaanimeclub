Shiba Inu Rooms TV Anime Announced for Summer 2027 With First Cast

The supernatural comedy Shiba Inu Rooms is getting a TV anime in summer 2027, with a teaser and initial cast and staff announced.

桜
USA Anime Club Editorial

Published November 14, 2026 · Updated November 14, 2026

THE STORY AT A GLANCE

The official adaptation announcement gives Shiba Inu Rooms a summer 2027 Japanese broadcast window, a teaser and initial cast including Niina Nakabayashi and Mutsumi Tamura.

A new adaptation on the schedule

The official Shiba Inu Rooms accounts announced on November 1 that Esu Omori's supernatural comedy manga is being adapted into a television anime for a summer 2027 broadcast in Japan. The announcement was accompanied by a teaser trailer, key visual and initial cast and staff information.

Crunchyroll's report, which embeds the official Japanese announcement, identifies TMS Entertainment as the animation studio and Osamu Yamasaki as director. It names Niina Nakabayashi as Kori Momose and Mutsumi Tamura as Mu, the Shiba Inu spirit at the center of the series. These are confirmed production details, not a US broadcast commitment.

What makes the premise distinctive

The story follows student Kori as she moves into an apartment occupied by Mu, an earthbound Shiba Inu spirit. Their awkward attempts at coexistence supply the supernatural-comedy premise. The original manga began as a Shonen Jump+ one-shot and entered serialization in 2024, with VIZ handling English manga publication.

The adaptation may interest readers who prefer gentler character comedy to action-centered fantasy, but it is too early to judge the series' pacing or final visual execution from an announcement trailer alone. Promotional footage and finished episodes can differ.

The US distribution question

The confirmed window is a Japanese summer 2027 broadcast. A statement about Japanese television does not establish a North American simulcast, English-dub cast, or premiere time on a US platform. None of those should be derived from the fact that English-language anime websites are covering the news.

We will treat any later US distribution announcement as a separate update. In the meantime, the published teaser and staff credits are the reliable details worth following, while VIZ's English manga provides a legally published path into the original story.

Primary source and reporting notes

Based on Shiba Inu Rooms official adaptation announcement as reported with source by Crunchyroll on November 1. We report the original announcement with context for US fans; details not confirmed by the source are identified as such.
