Albus Changes the World Makes Its US Manga Debut With Volume 1

VIZ's first English volume of Albus Changes the World arrived November 10, 2026. Here's the credited creative team and what readers can verify.

桜
USA Anime Club Editorial

Published November 14, 2026 · Updated November 14, 2026

THE STORY AT A GLANCE

VIZ lists Albus Changes the World Volume 1 for November 10, 2026, credited to writer Akihisa Maki and artist Miki Yatsubo, with a 200-page Teen-rated edition.

A new English series on the calendar

Albus Changes the World has a first English-language volume listed by VIZ Media for November 10, 2026. VIZ names Akihisa Maki for the story and Miki Yatsubo for the artwork, identifies the book as a Shonen Sunday title, and lists 200 pages with a Teen age rating. Its official page includes a free preview alongside purchase information.

A volume-one release matters differently from a long-running series reaching its next installment. It gives English-language readers an identifiable starting point without needing to reconstruct dozens of earlier collected books. The printed ISBN on VIZ's listing, 978-1-9747-6943-8, is useful when comparing local bookstores or catalog records.

The premise without unnecessary spoilers

VIZ describes Albus as a hero able to rewind time and restart after death. Even after finally defeating the Demon Lord and obtaining peace, he is unsatisfied: he wants to go back and accomplish the task perfectly. The premise takes a familiar fantasy time-loop device and asks what success means when it can always be redone.

That is a synopsis from the publisher, not our evaluation of the entire series. We have not independently reviewed the complete volume, so we are not giving a star rating or claiming how the plot resolves. Readers wanting to judge the tone and pacing should try the publisher's authorized preview.

How to find the right edition

Check the title, volume number, creative credits and November 10 publication date against VIZ's official entry. The listing is for a manga volume rather than an anime adaptation; it supplies no streaming service, animation studio or television premiere date.

Because this is a series debut, it is worth distinguishing the printed edition from any digital preview and from the original Japanese release. USA Anime Club will treat future adaptation rumors and sales claims separately unless backed by a named primary source.

Primary source and reporting notes

Based on VIZ Media's Albus Changes the World Volume 1 product listing. We report the original announcement with context for US fans; details not confirmed by the source are identified as such.
