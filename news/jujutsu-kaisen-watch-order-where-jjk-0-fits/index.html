Jujutsu Kaisen Watch Order: Where JJK 0 Fits and What to Watch First

Follow the clearest Jujutsu Kaisen viewing order, understand why JJK 0 is a prequel best watched after Season 1, and see how Season 3 fits the Culling Game story.

桜
USA Anime Club Editorial

Editor-in-Chief: Satya Prasad

Published November 20, 2026 · Updated November 20, 2026 at 10:15 AM UTC

QUICK ANSWER

The recommended first-watch order is Jujutsu Kaisen Season 1, the JJK 0 movie, Season 2, then Season 3. The film takes place before Season 1 and follows Yuta, but it was released after the first season and works especially well as a world-expanding bridge. Season 3 adapts the Culling Game and concluded its first part at episode 59 in March 2026. This guide distinguishes story chronology from the clearest release order.

Source record: November 20, 2026 · Read the original source

WHAT TO CHECK NEXT

Where does Jujutsu Kaisen 0 go?

Watch Season 1, then JJK 0, Season 2 and Season 3. The movie is a prequel but release order is the clearest first watch.

Best first-time watch order

1. Jujutsu Kaisen Season 1, episodes 1–24. 2. Jujutsu Kaisen 0, the feature film. 3. Season 2, episodes 25–47. 4. Season 3, episodes 48–59 (Season 3's first part, as indexed by the official site through its March 2026 finale). The exact numbering convention may differ on a streaming service, so look for the season label and episode title.

This release order gives newcomers the original introduction to Yuji and the central curse-fighting team before shifting attention to Yuta in the film. It then returns to familiar characters in Season 2 and reaches the Culling Game in Season 3. You could watch the prequel chronologically first, but release order preserves how the franchise introduces its lore and characters.

Why JJK 0 is set earlier but works after Season 1

Jujutsu Kaisen 0 follows Yuta Okkotsu and his first year at Tokyo Jujutsu High, before Yuji's story. That makes the movie a prequel in the in-universe timeline. It is also a complete film with its own emotional center, not a collection of missing episodes that you must insert into Season 1.

After Season 1, you already understand the basic idea of cursed energy, sorcerer training and the school setting. The film then adds another perspective and shows the organization from a different angle. Watching it before Season 1 is possible, but a new viewer may have less context for the terminology and may experience certain connections differently.

At a glance: chronology versus viewing order

IN-UNIVERSE CHRONOLOGY → JJK 0 → Season 1 → Season 2 → Season 3.

RECOMMENDED FIRST WATCH → Season 1 → JJK 0 → Season 2 → Season 3.

WHY THEY DIFFER → JJK 0 is a prequel, but the television story introduces the franchise's concepts first and the movie later broadens the cast. For most newcomers, release order is the more welcoming route.

Season 2 contains two major story sections

Season 2 opens with a past-era story centered on Satoru Gojo and Suguru Geto, then returns to the present for the Shibuya Incident. This split is intentional. Do not mistake the earlier timeline for a recap film or assume the season begins with a separate spinoff; both sections are part of the main television order.

If you are watching in a service that divides Season 2 into separate parts or uses a special listing, preserve episode order within the season. The official episode index is the reference point for numbered episodes and titles. Avoid jumping directly to Shibuya because the earlier part provides character history that changes how later scenes read.

What Season 3 covers as of November 2026

Season 3 adapts the Culling Game story. The official Japanese broadcast page says it began January 8, 2026, and the official March notice identifies episode 59 as Season 3 episode 12 and its first-part finale. The official episode index lists Season 3 and the film in the same franchise navigation, which is useful when a platform catalog separates movies from TV seasons.

That is a factual status check as of November 20, 2026, not a promise about the next season's timing. A future continuation date, episode count, English dub schedule or US simulcast time should be added only after an official production or distributor notice. The series's latest available installment can vary between Japanese broadcast, US streaming and dub schedules.

Do I need recap films or theatrical compilations?

Jujutsu Kaisen has had theatrical or compilation presentations around the franchise, including a recap of earlier events paired with new material in some markets. These listings can look like required new chapters even when their core content repeats the TV series. Read the official synopsis and release note: if the film is a recap, Season 1 and Season 2 remain the correct core path.

JJK 0 is different from a recap because it tells a full prequel story centered on Yuta. It is the one movie this order intentionally includes. If a distributor announces a new original film or a compilation with a significant new scene, describe it by its official label and explain whether its story is already covered in the episodic order.

A spoiler-light path for a returning fan

If you have seen Season 1 but skipped the film, watch JJK 0 before Season 2. If you finished Season 2, continue with Season 3 from its first episode; there is no need to replay the prequel unless you want a refresher. The central order remains straightforward even if your platform uses a different season grouping.

If you have only seen the film, the best next step is Season 1 episode 1, not Season 2. The movie introduces Yuta and key concepts but it does not retell Yuji's introduction, his relationships or the main TV plot. The shared world is not a substitute for the separate lead-character storyline.

The chronology is a useful answer to a story question, but it is not a rule for a first-time audience. Release order presents the setting in the same progression the original television audience encountered, then uses the film to deepen the world before Season 2 expands the conflict. If you have already watched the series out of order, there is no need to restart; simply watch the film before moving on to Season 2.

Where to stream in the US

US rights, subscription catalogs and dubs can change. Search the full title “Jujutsu Kaisen 0” separately from the Season 1 and Season 2 page; a service may not package the movie with the series. Confirm that the listing is the feature film rather than a trailer, recap or bonus entry.

Subtitles and English audio may be available on different dates or from different services. Check the player before you start a watch party so everyone has the same episode and version. This order is independent of provider: once you know which title corresponds to the movie and each season, follow the sequence above.

Quick answers

Should I watch JJK 0 before Season 1? You can, because it is a prequel, but Season 1 first is the recommended path for most newcomers. Where does it go? Between Season 1 and Season 2. What comes after Season 2? Season 3's Culling Game story. Is Season 3's first part complete? The official schedule says episode 59, Season 3 episode 12, aired March 26, 2026; check current US services for the dub and latest regional release.

Simple route: Season 1 → JJK 0 → Season 2 → Season 3. Use chronological order only if you deliberately want the events arranged by fictional timeline. For a first viewing, the original release sequence builds the world cleanly and gives the prequel room to feel like a meaningful expansion.
