Rascal Does Not Dream of a Dear Friend Sets February 2027 US Screenings

Crunchyroll and Sony announce February 21, 23 and 24 US screenings for Rascal Does Not Dream of a Dear Friend, with subtitle and dub options.

By USA Anime Club Editorial · Published November 14, 2026 · Updated November 14, 2026

In brief: The anime film is scheduled for US theatrical screenings on February 21, 23 and 24, 2027, in both Japanese-with-English-subtitles and English-dub formats.

The US dates and formats

Crunchyroll and Sony Pictures Entertainment have announced a limited set of North American screenings for Rascal Does Not Dream of a Dear Friend. The published schedule names February 21, 23 and 24, 2027, for theaters in the United States and Canada. The companies also listed Australia, where the release is scheduled for February 18.

US screenings are expected to offer both the original Japanese audio with English subtitles and an English-dub option. That distinction matters when choosing tickets: two screenings of the same film may use different audio formats. Theater availability, showtimes and format labels should be confirmed with the exhibitor before purchasing.

Where the film fits in the series

The film continues the Rascal Does Not Dream story rather than launching a standalone franchise. It follows earlier television and theatrical entries, so a first-time viewer may miss major character context by starting here. Crunchyroll's announcement lists the earlier series and films as part of its catalog at the time of publication, but licensing can change.

The movie debuted in Japanese theaters on October 16, 2026. Its US arrival in February 2027 is therefore a separate territorial theatrical release, not a new Japanese premiere. As is normal for limited anime screenings, a nationally announced date does not guarantee that every US market will have a participating cinema.

Practical booking checks

Before sharing a ticket listing, verify the film title, city, date and whether the screening is subtitled or dubbed. The announcement specifies three US dates; it does not establish ticket prices, the total number of screens or a future US streaming date. Those details should not be inferred from the theatrical notice.

For fans organizing a group visit, the most useful next step is to watch the official distributor's ticketing announcement and local theater listings. We will update this story when directly confirmed information becomes available, rather than treat unverified booking pages as final.

Primary announcement and verification

This report is based on Crunchyroll and Sony Pictures theatrical announcement, October 29, 2026. We summarize this publisher/distributor announcement and add US context; we do not claim a separate release confirmation.
