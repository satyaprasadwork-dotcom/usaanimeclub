Jujutsu Kaisen Season 4: What Is Confirmed and What Fans Are Still Waiting For

Jujutsu Kaisen Season 4 has been announced as Culling Game Part 2. Here's what's officially confirmed, what's not, and what US fans should watch next.

WHAT TO CHECK NEXT

Is a precise Jujutsu Kaisen Season 4 date announced?

Production of Culling Game Part 2 is confirmed, but use the official series updates rather than speculative day-and-hour claims.

Overview

When a major anime season ends in the middle of a larger conflict, the next announcement can create more questions than it answers. Jujutsu Kaisen is now in that position. Viewers know the story has unfinished business, and official material has confirmed a fourth television season, but an announcement of production should not be confused with a full release calendar.

Season 4 was officially announced in March 2026 under the description Culling Game Part 2. A teaser trailer followed in June, and the anime's official Japanese website published another promotional visual in August. Those developments establish that the continuation is real and actively promoted. They do not, on their own, establish a specific U.S. premiere date or hour.

What does “Culling Game Part 2” mean?

The Culling Game is one of the story's major later conflicts. The anime's third season began adapting that material, and the fourth is framed as its continuation. The description is important because it explains why the new installment belongs to the same broad storyline rather than starting an unrelated arc simply because it has a higher season number.

For viewers who have finished Season 3, the new label signals narrative continuation. For viewers who stopped after the Shibuya Incident, it is a warning that substantial developments stand between them and the fourth season. Jumping ahead could make alliances, stakes and rules feel arbitrary.

Different outlets may call the continuation “Season 4,” “Culling Game Part 2” or a combination of the two. Those expressions refer to the announced continuation, not evidence of three separate projects.

What is actually confirmed?

The official anime website has published promotional material for the fourth season, and Crunchyroll reported the March announcement and later teaser. That supports the existence of a new anime installment and its story designation. It also supports the fact that the production has begun sharing official imagery with the public.

A teaser is not a schedule. Short footage can confirm a character's appearance or a visual direction, but it cannot establish how many weekly episodes will air, which month the show will premiere or how long the English dub will trail the Japanese broadcast. Readers deserve a clear separation between those facts and predictions.

As of this update, we have not verified an official exact release date in the sources used. A headline promising a specific day without a directly attributable production or platform announcement should be treated with skepticism.

Will it stream on Crunchyroll in the United States?

Crunchyroll streams the previous Jujutsu Kaisen television seasons and Jujutsu Kaisen 0. That makes it an important place to follow the franchise, and its news operation has covered Season 4's announcement. However, availability of prior installments does not automatically establish the detailed U.S. license terms, schedule or audio options for a future season.

When Crunchyroll or the production committee publishes a specific Season 4 streaming plan, that is the moment to update the U.S. guide. Until then, don't transform a reasonable expectation into an official guarantee. In search results, “expected on Crunchyroll” and “confirmed by Crunchyroll for a particular date” mean different things.

If you want to catch up now, the existing episodes and film provide a much more concrete starting point than any fan-made release calendar.

What should viewers remember from the previous story?

At its core, Jujutsu Kaisen follows characters drawn into a dangerous system of curses, sorcerers and institutions whose decisions have enormous consequences. Yuji Itadori's story has never been only about gaining strength. He repeatedly has to deal with responsibility for outcomes he couldn't fully control, and the people around him disagree about what justice or duty requires.

The Shibuya Incident reshaped the story's sense of safety and its relationships. The Culling Game's framework raises different questions about survival, rules and competing aims. If you've not yet seen Season 3, those are reasons to watch it before searching for the fourth season's plot spoilers.

A genuine refresher should focus on why specific characters make the choices they do, rather than merely listing which techniques won which confrontations. The series rewards attention to motives and institutional pressures as much as to its intricate combat system.

Why a release date is hard to infer

Big anime adaptations have complicated production schedules. Staff changes, broadcast arrangements, international licensing and dubbing plans can all influence when a season reaches viewers. A previous gap between seasons may be useful historical context, but it is not an equation that produces the next release date.

It is also risky to infer the television schedule from the timing of a promotional event. A teaser in June and a visual in August show an active publicity cycle. They don't reveal whether the project will debut a few months or more than a year later.

For USA Anime Club, this is a good place to stand out from rumor sites. Put a small status line near the top: “Season 4 announced; exact premiere date not verified.” Link the latest official source and update the field when it changes. Readers returning to the page will know precisely why the claim can be trusted.

Does the finished manga reveal the anime's ending?

Gege Akutami's original Jujutsu Kaisen manga concluded in September 2024. Fans who read the entire manga know material that the television anime has not adapted. That creates a dangerous search environment for anime-only viewers. A result about a “Culling Game ending” may discuss scenes far beyond the forthcoming episodes.

A season preview should not take its structure from a spoiler-heavy fan wiki without warning. If a site wants to publish manga comparisons, those belong in clearly labeled articles. At the very least, avoid placing late-series character fates or outcomes in titles and search descriptions shown to everyone.

The existence of completed source material also doesn't prove where Season 4 will stop. Adaptation pacing is a production choice, and precise boundaries should be confirmed through episode coverage or explicit official descriptions.

What would make a useful next update?

The next editorial milestone is a dated official announcement stating the Japanese premiere, the U.S. streaming destination and whether the rollout is weekly. After that, details about audio languages, cast, staff and episode count can be added in a clearly separated section. If a trailer is released, describe what it actually shows without explaining its imagery through later manga spoilers.

For readers, a current information page is more useful than a speculative countdown. It allows the distinction between “confirmed season” and “confirmed date” to survive every social-media rumor cycle.

Is it worth catching up now?

Yes, if you enjoy the series and want to be ready when the next announcement arrives. But there is no need to rush simply because a countdown account has invented a deadline. Revisit the major character decisions and the rules introduced in the current storyline. If the manga interests you, read it through licensed services and choose consciously whether to cross the anime-spoiler boundary.

The more meaningful preparation is understanding the characters' positions at the end of the released season. That will do far more for your enjoyment than knowing the next premiere month a few weeks before everyone else.

Bottom line

Season 4 of Jujutsu Kaisen is real and officially identified with Culling Game Part 2. Announcements and promotional material have appeared throughout 2026. An exact U.S. premiere date, full episode count and detailed language schedule should not be treated as confirmed without a newer official source. Until those arrive, catch up on the existing anime and keep manga spoilers separate from anime news.
