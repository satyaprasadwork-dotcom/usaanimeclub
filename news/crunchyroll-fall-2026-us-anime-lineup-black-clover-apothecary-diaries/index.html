Fall 2026 US Anime Lineup: Crunchyroll Confirms Black Clover and Apothecary Diaries

Crunchyroll's Fall 2026 catalog includes Black Clover Season 2, The Apothecary Diaries Season 3 and more titles with North American availability.

By USA Anime Club Editorial · Published November 14, 2026 · Updated November 14, 2026

In brief: Crunchyroll's published Fall 2026 lineup confirms North American streaming for several major returning anime, including Black Clover Season 2 and The Apothecary Diaries Season 3.

Which returning shows are listed

Crunchyroll's official Fall 2026 seasonal lineup identifies Black Clover Season 2 and The Apothecary Diaries Season 3 among its featured returning series. The distributor also lists Aoashi Season 2 and a larger slate of new premieres and continuing programs. Importantly for this publication, the lineup gives territorial information for individual shows rather than implying every series launches in every country.

The Black Clover listing names Studio Pierrot and a Saturday subtitled simulcast slot at 8:00 a.m. Pacific Time. The Apothecary Diaries listing names OLM and a Friday subtitled slot at 8:00 a.m. Pacific Time. These are the published slots from the lineup and can change; the source should be checked for subsequent updates.

Why the US catalog matters

North American availability is confirmed for both lead titles in the distributor's announcement. That is more useful to US readers than treating a global promotional poster as proof of local licensing. Distribution territory and language version are separate questions: a title may have a regional streaming home before a dubbed release schedule is finalized.

Crunchyroll's separate fall English-dub announcements provide additional context, but the exact status of a particular title should still be checked on its current show page. Platform catalogs, dates and language options are operational facts that may change after an initial announcement.

How to read an evolving seasonal schedule

The original fall slate was posted on September 15 and updated on October 6, 2026. A seasonal lineup is a living reference, not a permanent guarantee that every episode and audio option will appear at the originally printed minute. It is useful for planning what to watch, while a show-specific schedule remains the better source for a particular day's release.

USA Anime Club is publishing this US-focused digest as a verified snapshot of the announced lineup, not as a claim that the service's entire current catalog has been independently retested. Readers should follow the linked platform source for the latest corrections and title additions.

Primary announcement and verification

This report is based on Crunchyroll Fall 2026 seasonal lineup, published September 15 and updated October 6. We summarize this publisher/distributor announcement and add US context; we do not claim a separate release confirmation.
