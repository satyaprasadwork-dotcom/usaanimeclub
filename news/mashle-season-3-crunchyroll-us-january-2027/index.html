MASHLE Season 3 Confirmed for Crunchyroll Streaming in January 2027

The Tri-Magicathlon Divine Visionary Final Exam Arc of MASHLE arrives in January 2027; Crunchyroll confirms US streaming rights.

By USA Anime Club Editorial · Published November 14, 2026 · Updated November 14, 2026

In brief: Crunchyroll will stream MASHLE Season 3 during its January 2027 premiere window, with the United States included in the announced territories.

Crunchyroll's distribution announcement

Crunchyroll confirmed on October 28 that MASHLE: MAGIC AND MUSCLES Season 3 will stream on the service beginning in January 2027. The new season carries the subtitle The Tri-Magicathlon Divine Visionary Final Exam Arc. The platform lists worldwide rights excluding Japan and China, which includes the United States.

That scope is unusually clear for a first licensing notice: American viewers can identify the announced destination without guessing whether the show will be available in their territory. The announcement stops short of publishing an exact American release time or a complete weekly episode calendar.

Returning production and story context

The new television season continues the adaptation produced at A-1 Pictures. According to the distributor, Tomonari Tanaka returns as director, with Yosuke Kuroda handling series composition and Masaru Yokoyama providing music. Chiaki Kozumi is named as the incoming character designer for the third season.

The show follows Mash Burnedead, a physically gifted student navigating a world where magical ability determines status. This installment advances the Divine Visionary examination storyline. The original manga by Hajime Komoto concluded in Japan in 2023; the official English edition is published by VIZ Media, independently of the anime streaming schedule.

What is not announced yet

The January window is confirmed, but readers should distinguish that from a confirmed exact date or same-day English dub. A streaming rights announcement also does not establish whether every audio language will launch together or whether episodes will be released simultaneously in all regions.

US fans can revisit the previous seasons on Crunchyroll while waiting for the platform's detailed winter schedule. For a release-time claim, check the show page or an official dated schedule rather than a countdown that cannot document its source.

Primary announcement and verification

This report is based on Crunchyroll's October 28, 2026 streaming announcement. We summarize this publisher/distributor announcement and add US context; we do not claim a separate release confirmation.
