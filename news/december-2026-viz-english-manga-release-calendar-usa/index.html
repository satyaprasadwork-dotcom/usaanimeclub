December 2026 Manga Releases: The New VIZ Editions Worth Knowing

VIZ's official December 2026 US schedule includes Dandadan Vol. 22, Ichi the Witch Vol. 6, One Piece Box Set 5 and Sakamoto Days Vol. 24.

桜
USA Anime Club Editorial

Published November 14, 2026 · Updated November 14, 2026

THE STORY AT A GLANCE

VIZ's December 2026 calendar identifies distinct release groups for December 1, 8, 15 and 22. Key entries include Dandadan 22 and Ichi the Witch 6 on December 1, One Piece Box Set 5 and Frieren 15 on December 8, and Sakamoto Days 24 on December 15.

A dated US manga release calendar, not a popularity ranking

VIZ's official December 2026 calendar is a useful starting point for US manga readers because it groups English book releases by planned publication day. The schedule shows different types of products, including individual paperback volumes, continuation books, a series debut and a large boxed collection. Those categories matter more than a vague list of the month's biggest manga: a first volume invites a new reader, while a twenty-fourth volume generally assumes familiarity with an ongoing story.

This article focuses on selected books that can be confirmed from the calendar, with attention to what readers should check before buying them. It does not assert that every item will be in stock at every shop or that every title has an English digital edition included in the same purchase. The publisher's dates can change and local retailer availability is a separate question. Treat the entries as a dated buying and discovery guide, not a guarantee of fulfillment.

December 1: Dandadan, Ichi the Witch and more

The December 1 group includes Dandadan Volume 22 and Ichi the Witch Volume 6, two books already attracting attention among readers following their digital chapters. VIZ's individual product pages identify Dandadan 22 as a 210-page mature-rated release from Yukinobu Tatsu, and Ichi the Witch 6 as a 200-page paperback with story by Osamu Nishi and art by Shiro Usazaki. Their shared date does not mean the books contain similarly recent serialized material.

VIZ also lists A Star Brighter than the Sun Volume 8, Blue Exorcist Volume 33, Kill Blue Volume 10, Otaku Vampire's Love Bite Volume 9, and other continuing volumes for December 1. Different series reach different points in their collection histories. A reader shopping for one should check the exact volume number and any age rating attached to that book. A cover design or an anime recommendation from a friend is not enough to establish which numbered edition belongs next on a shelf.

December 8: a major One Piece collection and other releases

The December 8 calendar entry for One Piece Box Set 5: Wano to Egghead represents a different purchasing decision from a normal single volume. VIZ's dedicated listing identifies a collection of Volumes 91–111, a double-sided color poster and a paper One Piece trading-card-game playsheet. Someone who already owns most of those 21 numbered volumes may be paying again for content. A collector without that part of the series might value the convenience of one package.

The same date includes Frieren: Beyond Journey's End Volume 15, Call of the Night: Paradise Arc, Fly Me to the Moon Volume 34 and other releases. These should not be combined into an unsupported claim that every popular manga series is beginning a new arc on December 8. The calendar supplies publication records, not reviews of unread books. Readers can use it to identify a title, then consult the individual publisher page for a synopsis, format, language and store options before making a decision.

December 15: Sakamoto Days and expanding series

December 15 is the official date given for Sakamoto Days Volume 24. Readers following Yuto Suzuki's weekly digital chapters should remember that a collected book is different from the latest serialized chapter. VIZ also lists Kingdom Volume 14, Magilumiere Magical Girls Inc. Volume 16, Haikyu!! 3-in-1 Edition Volume 11 and The Apothecary Diaries: Maomao's Notes on the Inner Palace Volume 2 among the day's releases. The titles span different styles of manga and different edition formats.

That variety creates an easy opportunity for accidental purchases. A 3-in-1 omnibus combines multiple underlying numbered books, whereas a standard Volume 14 or 16 generally represents one collected installment. The Apothecary Diaries edition also belongs to a specifically named manga line; a similarly named novel or another manga adaptation should not be assumed to share its exact content. When shopping, identify the full subtitle, publisher and format rather than relying on a familiar franchise name.

December 22 and books that may otherwise be missed

The calendar continues past the heavily promoted early-month books. VIZ lists Trillion Game Volume 11 and Zom 100: Bucket List of the Dead Volume 21 for December 22. The later date is relevant to gift orders and holiday shopping, but it should not be translated automatically into a promised delivery before a specific celebration. A retailer may need extra time for stock intake and shipping, especially when a release falls close to a major seasonal shopping period.

Readers looking for less obvious titles can scan the full calendar by week rather than treating the headline releases as a complete inventory. A large publisher schedules different kinds of stories for different audiences, and a site's personal interest in action or supernatural manga should not erase romance, drama or comedy entries. An editorial guide can surface useful records without declaring that every release is essential or independently reviewing material it has not read.

How to shop the calendar without buying the wrong edition

Begin by writing down the series name, volume number and date from VIZ's official release page. Then open the individual product listing to check the ISBN, format and exact credits. That step matters because a publisher calendar is an overview, while a retail record is designed to distinguish editions. It also prevents confusion between a paperback, digital book, omnibus and box set. If a retailer offers an import from another territory, the language and publication information may differ legitimately.

Next, compare final checkout costs and fulfillment policies rather than only the displayed list price. A preorder may have a shipping estimate later than the publisher's date, and a store can sell out after launch. Checking the retailer's own inventory is more reliable than assuming the calendar proves availability everywhere. For a collector already owning earlier volumes, a simple spreadsheet of missing numbers may be more valuable than a ranking of December's most discussed franchises.

What an accurate monthly update should do next

A December guide should be refreshed when VIZ officially changes a release date, adds an edition or provides more precise product information. It should not imply that a chapter released online in November has been collected inside a book launching in December unless the actual contents support that claim. Manga serialization, book manufacturing and retail distribution move on different timetables. Recording those differences gives readers a practical reason to consult a release calendar.

For the November 14 research check, the important dates are December 1 for Dandadan Volume 22 and Ichi the Witch Volume 6, December 8 for One Piece Box Set 5 and Frieren Volume 15, December 15 for Sakamoto Days Volume 24 and several continuing series, and December 22 for titles including Trillion Game and Zom 100. Use the official VIZ calendar as the master schedule and confirm each item before purchasing.
