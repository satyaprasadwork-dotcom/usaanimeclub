Editorial Policy

USA Anime Club reports on anime, manga, manhwa and online fiction with an emphasis on accurate dates, clear sourcing and usefulness to US readers. Our editorial standard is to separate what a source confirms from interpretation or information that has not been announced.

Story selection and sources

We prioritize announcements that change a reader's practical understanding: a licensed English edition, a confirmed adaptation, an official release window, a streaming arrangement or a documented industry development. We prefer dated statements from rights holders, publishers, distributors, broadcasters, platforms and public agencies. When a secondary report is used, its original announcement should be identified whenever available.

Press releases are sources, not independent product reviews. We attribute announced facts to the issuing organization and avoid presenting supplied promotional claims as separately established results.

Dates, geography and uncertainty

Japanese television broadcast dates, US streaming availability, print publication and digital serialization can follow different schedules. We identify the region, format and source date when they affect a claim. If a cast, platform, dubbing plan or exact premiere day is not confirmed, the article should say so rather than fill the gap with speculation.

Originality, images and editorial responsibility

Reports should add original explanation or comparison instead of simply reproducing an announcement. Source links support factual claims; external text, manga pages, screen captures and promotional images are not republished without permission or another applicable lawful basis. The article covers use original typographic graphics rather than official character key art.

Research and drafting tools may assist preparation, but a tool output is not evidence. Sources and claims still require editorial checking. We do not attribute firsthand attendance, interviews, reviews or testing to anyone who did not perform that work. Articles use the publication byline USA Anime Club Editorial unless a specific verified contributor is credited.

Corrections and commercial independence

Substantive factual mistakes should be corrected on the page, with a clear note where the change affects the article's meaning. Subsequent announcements are dated developments, not proof that an earlier accurate announcement was fabricated. See our Corrections Policy.

If sponsorship, affiliate links or paid placement are introduced, the relationship should be clearly disclosed. Payment does not guarantee favorable editorial coverage. Send source questions or feedback to satyaprasadwork@gmail.com.
