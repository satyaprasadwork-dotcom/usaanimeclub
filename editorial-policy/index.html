Editorial Policy

We distinguish verified announcements from unconfirmed claims. Our first choice is a dated primary statement from a distributor, production company, licensor, publisher or event organizer.

United States relevance

We check the announced US or North American territory, premiere dates, time zones, language versions and theater availability. A Japanese premiere is not automatically a US release.

Original reporting and attribution

We produce original summaries and context with direct links to source materials. We do not fabricate interviews, attendance, viewership figures or exclusives. Site artwork is original typographic editorial illustration, not unlicensed promotional art.

Corrections and independence

We update a substantive change's date and disclose material factual corrections. Sponsorship, if introduced, will be disclosed; payment cannot buy editorial inclusion. Contact satyaprasadwork@gmail.com or review our corrections process.
