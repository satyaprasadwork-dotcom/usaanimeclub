About USA Anime Club

An independent editorial home for US anime fans.

What this website is

USA Anime Club is an independent website publishing practical guides for people who watch anime, read manga, experiment with cosplay or want to meet other fans in the United States. It is an editorial project, not an official chapter of a studio, publisher, streaming service or convention. Our goal is to make common fan decisions easier: where to look for legally available shows, how to budget for an event, how to organize a welcoming discussion, and how to avoid confusing promotional claims.

What we publish

We focus on evergreen explainers and US-specific decision guides. We separate verified facts from suggestions, link to authoritative information where possible and encourage readers to recheck rapidly changing facts such as schedules, content catalogs, admission prices and location rules.

Our US anime press desk

We now accept relevant news tips and press releases from US distributors, manga publishers, convention teams, creators and local fan communities. A submission is not an accepted or published story. Supplied releases will be labeled and evaluated against official sources before publication, with sponsored material treated separately. Visit the press desk for what to send and how to contact us.

What we do not claim

The website does not currently advertise a calendar of confirmed public events or operate a working member registration system. We do not claim interviews, personal testing, attendance, community membership counts or partnerships that we cannot substantiate. We do not sell event tickets through the website.

Begin with something practical

Reading the site should lead to a useful next action. New viewers can start with a three-title shortlist, readers can check formats and ISBNs before buying, and first-time attendees can plan costs with the convention budget planner. These tools use information readers enter themselves; they do not claim live prices, ticket availability or tested products.

How the editorial desk works

Our publication byline is USA Anime Club. The project publishes explainers and original planning tools, with source links and a visible corrections route. Editorial preparation has included tool assistance; documented human source review is still required, as explained in our research and review notes. We do not attribute work to invented writers, claim to have attended events, or describe unperformed product comparisons as hands-on reporting.

Our current fifteen guides still require documented source-by-source editorial review. This is an open quality-control item, not a reason to assume their statements have all been independently verified. In the meantime, readers should check the current primary source for any price, availability, law or event rule. We welcome corrections that identify the specific claim and supporting evidence.
