Editorial Policy

How USA Anime Club researches, publishes and corrects articles.

Editorial purpose

We publish independent, practical information for anime fans in the United States. Content should answer a meaningful reader question with useful specifics, limitations and enough context to act on the advice. We avoid manufacturing event reports, first-hand product testing or personal experiences.

Research and attribution

Our articles prioritize official organizers, copyright owners, public agencies, libraries, accessibility resources and service help centers for factual claims. External links do not indicate endorsement. Where a claim is likely to change, we identify it as changeable and point to a source readers can check themselves. General guidance and editorial opinion are distinguished from law, policy and official rules.

Press releases and supplied material

USA Anime Club accepts relevant press releases and story ideas through its press desk. If a release is published, we identify it as supplied material rather than passing it off as original reporting. We review basic claims, rights, official links and US audience relevance, and publication is never automatic. Advertiser-funded items must be disclosed and must not be presented as independent news.

Originality and intellectual property

We aim for original writing and original visual assets. Anime titles and names are used descriptively, not to claim affiliation. We do not reproduce copyrighted episode footage, manga pages, artwork or promotional photography without permission or a documented lawful basis. If rights holders have a concern, our contact page offers a direct channel.

Corrections and updates

When an error is substantiated, we update the page, including dates where materially helpful, and correct the underlying claim. We do not quietly turn unverified guesses into asserted facts. Readers can send correction requests to satyaprasadwork@gmail.com with a URL and supporting evidence.

Editorial independence and monetization

Any future sponsorship, affiliate relationship or paid placement should be conspicuously disclosed. An advertising script does not mean the site or its content has been reviewed or endorsed by Google. Editorial recommendations should not be influenced by payments without an explicit disclosure. This website does not currently publish endorsements presented as hands-on tests.

Human editorial responsibility and review

USA Anime Club is a human-directed editorial project. Research and drafting tools, including AI assistance, have been used for portions of the current guides. Tool-assisted preparation is not a substitute for a human editor checking factual statements against the original source. Each guide remains marked as awaiting documented human sign-off until a named reviewer has actually checked it. We do not claim interviews, attendance, physical product tests or credentials that have not occurred. The research and review notes explain the verification process and current status.

Source and claim checks

Time-sensitive claims about streaming availability, conventions, licensing or prices must be checked against original providers as close to publication as possible. We label hypothetical calculations, distinguish general guidance from official policy and prefer direct source links over unsupported certainty. Our automated tests confirm structure and navigation, not whether every factual statement has been independently verified.

Publishing gate

New guides should remain unpublished drafts until the factual statements have been checked against exact source URLs, imagery rights are confirmed, examples are either original or explicitly hypothetical, and the reviewer records that work. An automated site check does not count as an editorial fact check.

Author attribution

Until individual contributors are identified and verified, articles are credited to USA Anime Club rather than inventing biographies or professional credentials. Editorial ownership and accountability are explained on this page and the About page.

How we label commercial relationships

Affiliate links and paid placements should be disclosed clearly if they are introduced. Recommendations must remain understandable without clicking an advertisement or affiliate offer. The current guide collection does not include an affiliate store, a merchandise checkout or claimed sponsor relationships. We do not place advertising on 404, empty, or navigation-only pages, and ad placement will remain subject to approval and privacy requirements.
