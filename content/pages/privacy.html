Privacy Policy

How website analytics, ads and contact emails relate to your privacy.

Last updated: November 3, 2026. This notice explains the code published with this website. Actual hosting arrangements, log retention, cookies and region-specific legal requirements still need to be checked against the live providers and website operator.

What this website collects

There is no membership registration form, online payment processing or on-site public comment form. If you email us, the email provider processes your address and message so we can read and respond. The hosting provider may record routine server logs such as IP address, request time and browser information for security and service delivery, according to its own practices.

Optional measurement

The website offers a choice before loading optional Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-G0TNMR0FQL) and Microsoft Clarity (project ID yyqq9cryjc). If you select “Accept analytics,” these services may use browser storage and receive usage information as described in their policies. If you decline, this site does not intentionally initialize those optional measurement integrations. A preference is stored locally in your browser to remember your choice.

Advertising

The website includes a Google AdSense loader associated with publisher ID ca-pub-7937216909291905, for site verification and prospective advertising. Site approval and active ad serving are not guaranteed. Google or its partners may process technical data when advertising is enabled. For jurisdictions requiring a certified consent management platform (CMP), a compliant CMP must be configured before serving personalized ads; this site's basic analytics choices are not a substitute for such a CMP.

Changing your preference

Use the “Privacy choices” button in the footer to reopen the analytics preferences and change your decision. Revoking analytics on the site stops future initialization on subsequent page loads but does not retroactively erase measurements already sent. Clear browser site data to remove stored preferences and consider provider-specific privacy tools where appropriate. You can also adjust tracking preferences through browser controls. Clearing cookies or storage may remove the saved choice.
