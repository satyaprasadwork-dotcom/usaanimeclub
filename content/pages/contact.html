Anime news and press submissions

For US anime announcements, press releases, interviews, creator projects or convention story ideas, see our press submission guidelines. Editorial acceptance is not guaranteed, and we do not claim independent verification of unreviewed submissions.

Help us resolve a correction

If you report an issue, please include the article URL, the section or sentence concerned and a credible source supporting the correction. For accessibility issues, a description of the device, assistive technology or browser can help, but is optional.

Privacy and rights

Do not include passwords, financial details, personal identification numbers or private information about other people. If you have a copyright or trademark concern, identify the specific page and material. Our privacy notice describes how contact emails may be handled.

We do not publish a guaranteed response time, telephone number, physical office or public meeting schedule that we have not verified.
