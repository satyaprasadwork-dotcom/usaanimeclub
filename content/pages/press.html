The US fan desk · Media & community submissions

Have an anime story? Send it to the desk.

USA Anime Club welcomes relevant press releases and story tips about anime culture in the United States. Our current site includes independent fan guides; this page is an open submission channel, not a claim that a newsroom publishes daily breaking news.

01 / Announcements

Releases & premieres

Licensed US streaming launches, theatrical releases, home video and dubbing announcements. Include the official publisher or distributor source and the US availability details.

02 / Industry

Manga & creators

US manga publishing, original comics, artist projects and verified creator interviews. Tell us what's new and why it matters to American readers.

03 / Community

Conventions & clubs

US anime conventions, local meetups and fan initiatives. Include verified dates, location, organizer contacts, accessibility information and the official event page if available.

What to include

Send a clear subject line, the organization or creator's name, the actual announcement, the relevant US city or audience, verifiable dates and links to the original source. Include an approved image and its usage rights if available, along with a media contact we can use for fact-checking. We may ask clarifying questions or decline a submission.

How we handle press releases

A release is a statement supplied by an interested party, not an independently reported article. If one is published, it should be clearly labeled as a press release or as supplied material, and any substantive factual claims should be checked against official sources where feasible. Edited reporting, original fan guides and sponsored content are different content types; we will not disguise advertisements as news.

Editorial independence and rights

Sending a release does not guarantee publication, endorsement, a link, or a particular posting date. Do not send confidential materials or artwork you are not authorized to share. If a proposal involves payment, it must be disclosed and appropriately labeled, and any links must follow applicable search engine guidance. See our editorial policy and independent fan site notice.

Contact the editorial desk

Email contact@usaanimeclub.com with the subject USA Anime Club story submission. The site does not currently offer an upload portal, membership account, or public event calendar. For ordinary questions and corrections, use our contact page.
