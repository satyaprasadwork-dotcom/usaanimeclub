First-episode method

Make a three-title shortlist and check official descriptions, age information, legal US availability and audio or subtitle choices. Try one episode and decide whether the mood, pacing and story work for you before committing to a long series.

What the estimate means

Total minutes = episodes × estimated episode length. Weeks = total minutes ÷ your available weekly minutes, rounded up. Reality can change with extra-length episodes, breaks, rewatching and your schedule.
