USA Anime Club

Community Guidelines

Expectations for respectful conversations and safe fan activities.

A welcoming fandom

We want anime discussion to be accessible to newcomers and longtime viewers alike. Treat other people as people. Do not harass, threaten, stalk, impersonate or deliberately humiliate others over series preferences, subtitles, dubs, genre choices or knowledge of a franchise.

Spoilers and attribution

Clearly warn before discussing major plot turns in shared spaces. Credit fan artists, writers, costume makers and photographers. Seek permission before reposting their work or pictures of other people. Do not share pirated streams, stolen material or links designed to evade paywalls or licensing.

Safety and privacy

Never pressure someone to disclose a real name, location, age, contact information or private account. For meetups, use organizer-verifiable locations, visible event rules and safety arrangements. Respect accessibility needs, personal boundaries and photography consent. Harassment and discrimination are not accepted.

Minors

Any future real-world activities involving minors need clear supervision, permission and safeguarding arrangements. The website currently has no public member directory or active event registration. Do not assume an unverified online invitation is run by USA Anime Club.

Moderation and reporting

We do not currently host a public commenting platform. If a related activity or future community feature is launched, its moderation approach and reporting mechanism should be published before opening it. For concerns about content on this website, contact contact@usaanimeclub.com.
